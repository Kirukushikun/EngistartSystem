Project Initialization System

A short briefing on why every farm project request now moves through one system — before we hand it over to a live walkthrough.

01 — The Reality

Some friction was never anyone's fault.

A request that has to pass through several places will always take time and cross hands — that's not a flaw to fix, it's what a real approval chain looks like. What made it hard was running that chain on paper, by hand, with coordination scattered across group chats.

01

The requestor filled out a physical form, then personally carried it — office to office — to get it approved by Division Head and VP Gen Services.

02

Once approved, it was forwarded to two people at once for acceptance — what's now two separate, accountable steps, handled together out of necessity.

03

From there, coordination moved into a group chat — the meeting, the inspection, all of it — separate from the form itself.

04

And if the form was misplaced anywhere along that trip, the request started over from scratch — printing included.

02 — The Shift

One request. One record. One path — from submission to the field.

Every role sees the same request, at the same status, with the same history — no matter where it entered the chain.

The same five checkpoints, the same people — just systemized. Nothing about who reviews what has changed. What changed is what it takes to move a request from one to the next.

One click, not one trip

What used to mean printing, hand-carrying, and waiting for someone to be at their desk is now a single click, from wherever you are.

Recorded, and easy to trace

Every step leaves a record — no relying on memory, a stack of paper, or a message thread to reconstruct what happened.

Transparent by default

No need to flood a chat thread to stay in the loop — status and remarks are visible instantly, to any role that needs them.
